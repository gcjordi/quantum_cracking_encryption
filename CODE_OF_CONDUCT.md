# Code of Conduct

I want this repository to remain a professional, technical and constructive place for collaboration.

Contributors and participants are expected to communicate respectfully, focus criticism on ideas and code, avoid harassment or personal attacks, respect privacy, and keep security-sensitive information out of public threads when responsible disclosure is appropriate.

Technical disagreement is welcome when it is evidence-based and relevant to the project. Deliberate misrepresentation of the project's educational scope, abusive behavior, spam, or attempts to use the project community to facilitate unauthorized attacks may be moderated or removed.

As maintainer, I may edit, hide, lock or remove contributions or participation that materially violates these expectations.
