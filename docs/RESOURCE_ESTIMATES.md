# RSA-2048 quantum resource context

These figures are **research estimates under explicit assumptions**, not forecasts.

## 2019

Gidney & Ekerå estimated that RSA-2048 could be factored in about eight hours using about 20 million noisy physical qubits under their stated fault-tolerance assumptions.

Reference: https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.09749

## 2025

Craig Gidney published a substantially revised estimate: RSA-2048 in less than one week using fewer than one million noisy physical qubits under the paper's assumptions, enabled by newer arithmetic and error-correction techniques.

Reference: https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.15917

The large change between estimates is itself important: quantum-threat resource estimates depend heavily on algorithms, architecture and error-correction assumptions and should always be dated and sourced.
